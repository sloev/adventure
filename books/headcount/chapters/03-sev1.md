:: s3-tobi

Hasenheide is the park at the end of your street, a long tangle of old trees and paths and slopes between Neukölln and Kreuzberg, with a petting zoo and a dilapidated open-air cinema and an artificial hill made of wartime rubble, and men along the main path who sell weed to tourists and say *alles gut?* to everyone who walks past. "The brake? The screaming one?" He almost smiles. "Everyone on the floor knows that brake. Ben says he can hear you coming from the Oberbaum bridge."

"And I can't—" He stops. He takes his hood down, and rubs his face with both hands. "I can read it. I can tell you what every line does. But I can't tell you if it's *right*. You know?
