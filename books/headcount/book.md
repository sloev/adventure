Berlin, January. You are Jakob Lenz, forty-one, senior backend engineer on the Settlements team at Kontor, an invoicing company of four hundred people in a converted brick factory in Kreuzberg. You know where the old code is buried. You have a daughter starting school in August and a rent that went up again.

This is the year the company becomes *AI-native*. Over twelve months of standups, coffee-machine talk, kicker matches, Kantine lunches, retros, pull requests, all-hands and 3 a.m. pages, you will decide what kind of colleague you are when the arithmetic of headcount stops being abstract.

Nothing dramatic happens in this book that does not happen in offices every day.
