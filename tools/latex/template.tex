{\centering\large How to read this book\par}\vspace{6mm}
\noindent This is not an ordinary novel. You read it section by section. Most of the time a section flows straight into the next one, and you read on as you would in any book. When you reach a choice, pick one and turn to the numbered section it names. Section numbers are printed at the top of each section, and the outer corner of every page shows which sections it holds.

%%SESSIONS%% A session ends at a natural stopping place. Put the bookmark in, and come back tomorrow.

Sometimes a section asks you to note a \textbf{codeword}. Write it on the codeword sheet at the front of the book. Later sections may ask whether you have it. Codewords are how the book remembers who you have been, so be honest with it.
